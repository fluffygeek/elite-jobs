Elite Jobs — Employee Guide

How Elite Jobs Works

A simple guide to the app that tracks fiber and bore jobs. No computer experience needed — just read it top to bottom.

01

What Is Elite Jobs?

Elite Jobs is where we write down every job — where it is, what was done, and how much fiber or bore was used. It takes the place of the old spreadsheet. It works on your phone, even out in the field with no signal.

There are two kinds of people who use it:

Field Techs

You fill out a job after you finish the work — right from your phone.

Office Staff

You check each job, fix mistakes, flag problems, and close jobs out.

02

The Life Of A Job

Every job follows the same path, no matter which town it's in. Here's the whole trip, start to finish:

    A["Tech does the work"] --> B["Tech submits the job\nfrom their phone"]
    B --> C{"Office Staff\nchecks it"}
    C -->|"Looks good"| D["Marked Closed-Out"]
    C -->|"Something's off"| E["Flagged for follow-up"]
    E --> F["Office Staff fixes\nor asks the tech"]
    F --> C
Good to know No matter what town the job is in — Live Oak, Florida, Georgia — the steps are exactly the same.

03

For Field Techs: Sending In A Job

Do this every time you finish a job. It only takes a minute.

  1. 1

    Open Elite Jobs on your phone

    Log in with your own account. You'll only need to do this once — after that, your phone remembers you.

  2. 2

    Tap "New Job"

    You'll see a short form. It works the same even if you have no signal — your phone saves it and sends it the moment you're back online.

  3. 3

    Pick your town and fill in the details

    Job number, the address, how much fiber or bore you laid, and check the boxes that apply (Locate, Directional Bore, Prebury).

  4. 4

    Tap Submit

    That's it. The app figures out the payment code for you — you never have to do that math by hand.

No signal? No problem If you're somewhere with no bars, still fill out the job and hit Submit. Your phone holds onto it and sends it in automatically the next time it finds a signal. It will never make a copy or lose your job.

04

For Office Staff: Checking A Job

Open the dashboard to see every job from every town, all in one place.

  1. 1

    Find the job in the list

    New jobs show up as soon as the tech sends them in.

  2. 2

    Fix anything that's wrong

    You can change one thing — like the address — without touching anything else. If a coworker is editing that same job at the same time, the app won't let your changes clash. It will just ask you to try again.

  3. 3

    Flag it if something looks off

    Wrong address? Looks like a repeat job? Turn on the Discrepancy Flag so it's easy to find later.

  4. 4

    Close it out when it's done

    Once everything checks out, mark it Closed-Out. Techs can never do this step — only office staff can.

Need the data somewhere else, like for accounting? There are two export buttons: one pulls every job, the other pulls only the ones you've flagged.

05

Words You'll See In The App

Job Site

The state and zip code for the job. You don't type this — the app figures it out from the address.

Bore Code

The payment code for bore work, like DDB4 DBC1 x 300. The app works this out for you from the footage — you never calculate it by hand.

Discrepancy Flag

A little marker office staff can put on a job that needs a second look.

Closed-Out

Means the job is fully done and processed in the office. Only office staff can mark this.
